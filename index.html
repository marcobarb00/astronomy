An interactive course in classical positional astronomy

Predicting the Sky

By the end you can compute where the Sun, the Moon and a planet are on a given date, using geometric models you derived yourself, and you know how wrong each model is and why.

Every page either adds a piece to that machine or removes an error the previous piece left behind; a running error curve shows the model against the real sky, page after page.

Sources: Ptolemy's Almagest, Copernicus' De Revolutionibus, R. Fitzpatrick's A Modern Almagest, J. Meeus' Astronomical Algorithms and E. M. Standish's planetary elements (JPL). Pages marked in preparation are not written yet.
